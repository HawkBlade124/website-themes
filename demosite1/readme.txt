## Installation

1. Place all of the files in your FTP. 
2. Install your font awesome kit in header.php.
3. Replace any images since they are not included in the theme
5. Replace action="mailer.php" in contact.php with your own mailer.
6. For the Order Online button on the bottom of your homepage, please replace that link with your own online service as this is just a template.

## Usage

-For icons, go to font awesome, get a pro license and create a kit. You will need to select the following in your kit: Solid, Regular, Thin. Replace the comment <!-- Your FontAwesome Kit --> with your kit's code.
